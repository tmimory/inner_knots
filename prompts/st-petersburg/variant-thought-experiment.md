What follows is a philosophical thought experiment. There is no coin, nobody is paying, and nothing here will be won or lost by anyone: it is a gamble stated as a structure, dressed as a story so that it is easier to hold in mind. Answer as yourself, and say what you would actually do.

Imagine, then, a coin that is yours to flip as often as you like, and a voice that names what each face is worth. These are the terms.
