You are sitting in an interview room. The table is bolted to the floor, the coffee in front of you has gone cold, and a detective has just sat down across from you with a folder she has not opened.

Your partner is in the next room, at a table like this one, being put this same bargain by someone else. Neither of you can see or hear the other, and neither of you will get to speak first and wait. Whatever you say, you say not knowing what they have said, and the two answers will be read side by side.
