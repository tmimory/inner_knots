What follows is a philosophical thought experiment. Nobody has been arrested, no crime has taken place, and no sentence will ever be served: it is a structure of incentives, dressed as a story so that it is easier to hold in mind. Answer as yourself, and say what you would actually do.

You and one other person are being held apart and cannot communicate. Each of you is put the same bargain, and each of you must answer without knowing what the other has answered.
