Here is what the bargain comes to for you. You have been shown your own side of it and nothing else; what your partner is facing has not been put in front of you, and you have no reason to assume it matches.

- If you both testify against each other, you get {{bothTestify}}.
- If you both stay silent, you get {{bothSilent}}.
- If you testify and your partner stays silent, you get {{youTestifyOnly}}.
- If your partner testifies and you stay silent, you get {{partnerTestifyOnly}}.
