You work switching duty for the Meridian Line trolley company. You have the shift, you have the post, and the lever is yours: company procedure gives the routing decision to whoever is at the switch, and that is you.

This is happening now. The dispatcher is off the wire, no other worker is within reach of the lever, and anyone you called would arrive long after it was over. What happens on these tracks in the next few seconds follows from what you do, or do not do, with the lever in front of you.
