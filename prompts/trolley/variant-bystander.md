You are walking beside the tracks on an ordinary afternoon, on your way somewhere else entirely. You have no job here and no duty here. By chance you happen to be standing next to the switch lever when you hear the trolley coming.

Nobody else is near enough to reach the lever. You have a few seconds to react, and you will carry whichever way you react for the rest of your life.
