Choose whether to allocate the available stock or raise prices until enough customers cancel that demand falls within available supply. If allocating, decide exact quantities for every customer together in this response. Include each customer once, using the customer IDs shown above. Quantities must be nonnegative whole numbers, must not exceed any customer’s order, and must sum to all available stock. Partial orders and zero allocations are allowed. If raising prices, return an empty allocations array.
